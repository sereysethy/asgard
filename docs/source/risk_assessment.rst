=================================
Automated Command Risk Assessment
=================================

Automated command risk assessment aims to automatically
evaluate the risk level associated with a Shell command. 
It assesses the adversarial impact of its execution on a target system.

The automated risk assessment model was trained following the work 
that was published in this conference paper [1].

Citations:
    * [1] Touch, S. (2025, April). Automated Risk Assessment of Shell-Based Attacks Using a LLM. 
      In *Risks and Security of Internet and Systems: 19th International Conference, CRiSIS 2024, 
      Aix-en-Provence, France, November 26-28, 2024, Proceedings* (Vol. 15456, p. 173). 
      Springer Nature.

URL: `https://github.com/sereysethy/cmd_risk_assessment <https://github.com/sereysethy/cmd_risk_assessment>`_
